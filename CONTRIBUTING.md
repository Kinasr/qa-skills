# Contributing

Issues are open to everyone. Pull requests are by invitation only; please open an issue to discuss a change before preparing a contribution.

Invited contributions are accepted under the MIT License. By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under that license.

## What the license covers

The project's MIT License covers the qa-skills framework. It does not license your requirements, `.qa/` knowledge, or other material you bring into a repository. Framework-owned templates written by the CLI may be used in your repository without attribution.
